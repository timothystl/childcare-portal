# Parent User Manual
## Timothy Lutheran Church — Mother's Day Out

---

## Overview

The MDO portal lets you register your children for care days, view your current schedule, and join the enrollment waitlist. There are two main pages you will use:

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Registration page** (`index.html`) | Add care days for your children |
| **My Schedule** (`lookup.html`) | View your registered days and print your schedule |

You will need your **parent email address** and your **4-digit PIN** for both pages. If you do not know your PIN, contact the office or find it in your ProCare account.

---

## Part 1 — Registering for Care Days

### When Can I Register?

The registration window is normally **open during the first 20 days of each month**. After the 20th, the window closes while the office finalizes the next month's roster. You will see a banner at the top of the page telling you whether registration is currently open or closed.

---

### Step 1 — Find Your Family

1. Open the registration page.
2. Under **Find Your Family**, enter your **parent email address**.
3. Enter your **4-digit PIN**.
4. Click **Find My Family**.

If your information is correct, a green confirmation bar will appear showing your family name. If you get an error, double-check your email spelling and PIN. After **5 failed attempts** the system will lock you out temporarily — wait a few minutes and try again, or call the office.

> **Can't find your family?** Your account must already be set up by the office before you can log in. If you are new, use the **Join the Waitlist** button on the page, or contact the office directly.

---

### Step 2 — Select Your Children

After your family is verified, a list of your children appears.

- **Check the box** next to each child you want to register.
- All children you select in this step will share the same care days. If your children need **different schedules**, register them one at a time: select Child A, pick their days, and submit; then start over for Child B.

---

### Step 3 — Select Care Days on the Calendar

Once you have selected at least one child, the calendar becomes active.

**Reading the calendar:**

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| White / available dot | Spots are open — you can select this day |
| Yellow / limited dot | 3 or fewer spots remain |
| Gray / full dot | No spots available — this day is full |
| Blue / selected dot | You have selected this day |
| Blocked / crossed out | The program is closed (holiday, snow day, etc.) |

**Selecting days:**

- Click any available date. A small popup appears asking whether you want **Full Day** or **Half Day** — the price for each option is shown in the popup. Click your choice to confirm the selection. The day turns blue.
- If all of your selected children are in a full-day-only room, the Half Day option will not appear.
- If you are registering more than one child at the same time, the popup shows the combined price for all selected children.
- Click a blue (already selected) date to remove it from your selection.
- Use the month navigation to move forward and back in the calendar.

**Tips:**

- You can select days across multiple months in a single submission.
- Weekends and program closure dates cannot be selected.
- If a day shows as full, it is already at capacity. You can still register for other open days.

---

### Step 4 — Review Your Selection

Scroll down to the **Review Selection** section. It shows a summary of every date you have chosen, grouped by child. Check this carefully before submitting.

If you need to make changes, scroll back up to the calendar and click any selected date to remove it.

---

### Step 5 — Submit

Click **Submit Registration**. A confirmation screen will appear summarizing what was registered.

> Your registration is complete once you see the green confirmation screen. You do not need to do anything else — the office will see your registration automatically.

---

## Part 2 — Viewing Your Schedule

### How to Access My Schedule

Click the **📅 My Schedule** button (floating at the bottom-right of the registration page), or go directly to `lookup.html`.

1. Enter your **parent email address**.
2. Enter your **4-digit PIN**.
3. Click **Look Up My Schedule**.

Your registered care days will be displayed, organized by child and by month.

### Printing Your Schedule

Click **🖨️ Print** in the top-right corner of the schedule view to print or save your schedule as a PDF.

### Registering for Additional Days

From the schedule page, click **Register for additional days →** at the bottom to return to the registration page.

### Logging Out

Click **← Log Out** in the top-right corner when you are done.

---

## Part 3 — Changing or Cancelling Days

The online portal allows you to **add** care days. It does not allow you to remove or change days you have already registered for.

To **remove a day, switch a day, or make any other changes**, contact the office directly. An administrative fee of $5 may apply to changes made after the initial registration.

---

## Part 4 — Joining the Waitlist

If your family is not yet enrolled, or if the days or room you need are full, you can join the enrollment waitlist.

1. Click **⏳ Join the Waitlist** (available as a button on the registration page and as a floating button).
2. Fill in your contact information, your child's name and date of birth, and your care preferences (desired start date, days needed, full day or half day).
3. If a sibling is already enrolled at Timothy Lutheran MDO, check that box and provide their name and room — sibling families are prioritized.
4. Add any relevant notes and click **Join Waitlist**.

You will receive a confirmation on-screen. The office will contact you when a spot opens that matches your needs.

> You can apply to the waitlist even before your child is born — check the **"Child not yet born (expecting)"** box and enter the expected due date instead.

---

## Part 5 — Contacting the Office

Use the **💬 Contact Us** button (floating at the bottom-right of the registration page) to send a message to the office at any time. Fill in your name, email, and message, then click **Send Message**.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I forgot my PIN.**
Contact the office. They can look up or reset your PIN.

**The registration calendar is blank / nothing is selectable.**
The registration window may be closed for this month. Check the banner at the top of the page for the current status.

**I can see some days but not others.**
Days shown in gray are full. Days that are blocked out are program closures (holidays, snow days, etc.).

**I registered but don't see the days on My Schedule.**
Your registration was submitted but may take a moment to appear. Refresh the My Schedule page. If it still does not show, contact the office.

**My child's name is not showing up in Step 2.**
Your child must be added to the family directory by the office before they appear. Contact the office to have your child added.

**I need different days for two of my children.**
Register them separately. Complete Steps 2–5 for the first child, then start over and complete Steps 2–5 for the second child.
