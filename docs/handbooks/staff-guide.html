Start here

Where to go. The Staff app lives at mdo.timothystl.org/staff.html. Use your own phone — there is no shared tablet or wall device. Save it to your home screen for a one-tap launch.

The staff app has five tabs: Room, Schedule, Messages, Count (head count), and Account. Room is where you'll spend most of your day — it's the roster for whichever room you're clocked into, and the quick-log sheet you use to record each child's day.

Clocking in and out happens in a separate app. Staff.html is for logging care — meals, naps, diapers, messages, and schedules. To clock in or out for pay, use mdo.timothystl.org/clockin.html instead. See the separate Clock In/Out Guide.

Sign in and choose your room

  1. Choose your name from the dropdown — identity comes before the PIN, so a guessed PIN alone can't get in.
  2. Enter your PIN and tap Sign In.
  3. On the room picker, tap the room you're working in today. You can switch rooms later with ← Rooms at the top of the roster.

Your room roster

  1. ← Rooms takes you back to switch to a different room.
  2. Each child's card shows their name, current status (In, Out, or Not in), and any allergy or dietary chip on file. Tap a card to open the quick-log sheet for that child.
  3. Refresh pulls the latest roster — useful right after a child is dropped off in another room and moved, or after another teacher logs an event.

Logging a child's day

  1. Tap a child's card on the roster to open their quick-log sheet. Any allergy note appears at the top — always read it before a snack or meal.
  2. Tap Check in when the child physically arrives, and Check out when a parent or approved contact picks them up. This is the official arrival/departure record — it's what the parent app's Today tab reads.
  3. Tap the buttons for what happened — meal amount, diaper change, nap start/end, or a supplies request. You can tap more than one before posting.
  4. Tap Post to save. The button shows a count of what's queued so you can review before it goes out.
Log as it happens, not all at once at the end of the day. Parents follow along on Today in real time, and a same-day incident notice needs to reach the family and the director quickly.

My schedule and time off

  1. Open Schedule to see your shifts for the current week — start and end times, or "Off" for a day you're not scheduled.
  2. This shows only your own hours. Other teachers' schedules, pay, and time off aren't visible here.
  3. To request a day off, use the Ask for a day off flow — see the Clock In/Out Guide, since that request is submitted from the clock-in app. It's sent to the director for approval and your schedule doesn't change until she responds.

Messages with families

  1. Open Messages to see threads from families whose child is on your room's roster today — you won't see another room's messages.
  2. Tap a thread to read it and reply. Keep replies focused on the child and the day — for anything about billing, enrollment, or a policy question, point the family to the office.
For an urgent, safety-related, or same-day pickup issue, don't rely on a written message alone — speak with the office directly.

Head count (fire drills & emergencies)

  1. Open Count during a fire drill or any evacuation.
  2. Read the banner at the top — it tells you whether the count is built from today's check-ins or from booked care days (used as a fallback if no check-ins have been recorded yet that day).
  3. Check off each child as you physically account for them outside.
This screen exists for the moment it actually matters. It's worth opening once when it's calm, just so the layout is familiar before you ever need it under pressure.

Incident reports & missing child alerts

Filing an incident report (a fall, a scrape, a bite, or anything similar) is done from the sheet the app opens for that situation — fill in what happened, where, what was done, and who else saw it. Filing the report is your signature; the parent signs on your phone at pickup, and the director signs last before the family can read the full report in their portal.

If a child cannot be found, use the missing-child alert. It pushes a banner to every staff phone in the building at once — there's no "send to one person" option, on purpose. Every available adult should stop and help search immediately.

Notifications matter here. Turn on push notifications for the staff app in Account so a missing-child alert reaches your phone even if the app isn't open.
