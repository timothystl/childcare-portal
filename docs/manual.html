PART A — Staff & Admin Guide

A1 · Getting In

Admin Portal

  1. Go to admin.html on your MDO site.
  2. Enter your admin email address and password, then click Sign In.
  3. Your session stays active for 30 minutes of inactivity, then logs out automatically.
Only staff with an admin account can access this portal. Contact your developer to add or remove admin accounts.

Staff Clock-In Kiosk

  1. Go to clockin.html on a shared device (tablet or computer at the front desk).
  2. Staff enter their personal 4-digit PIN and tap Clock In or Clock Out.
  3. Each punch is recorded with a timestamp. Multiple shifts per day are supported.
  4. Staff PINs are set in the Staffing tab — see section A8.

A2 · Classrooms Tab

Use this tab to see who is enrolled on any given day — your daily attendance roster.

Viewing the Daily Roster

  1. Click the Classrooms tab.
  2. Pick a date using the date picker.
  3. Optionally choose a specific room from the room filter (leave blank to see all rooms).
  4. Click View Roster.
  5. Children are grouped by room. Each row shows the child's name, day type (Full or Half), and the rate charged.

Exporting / Printing the Roster

  1. After generating the roster, click Export Roster.
  2. A print-formatted page opens in a new window.
  3. Use your browser's print dialog (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) to print or save as PDF.
The roster only shows confirmed registrations — waitlisted children do not appear.
Requires developer: changing room capacity numbers or age range boundaries.

A3 · Care Calendar Tab

This tab shows enrollment across the full month as a grid per room. Green cells are open care days. Click any cell to see who is enrolled that day.

Registration Window Override

By default the enrollment window opens automatically at 9 AM on the 15th of each month. You can override this:

  1. Find the Registration Window dropdown in this tab.
  2. Select Force Open to open enrollment early, or Force Closed to pause it.
  3. Click Save.
  4. When you're ready to go back to the normal schedule, select Auto and click Save again.
Leaving it on Force Open means enrollment never closes. Always set it back to Auto when done.

Adding a Closure (Holiday / Snow Day)

  1. Scroll to the Closures section in this tab.
  2. Enter the date and an optional reason (e.g. "Thanksgiving Break").
  3. Click Add Closure. The day is immediately blocked on the parent calendar.
  4. To remove a closure, find it in the list and click Remove.

Notifying Parents of a Closure

  1. Enter the closure date and reason as above.
  2. Click 🔔 Notify Parents.
  3. A push notification is sent to all parents who have notifications enabled on their device.
Push notifications only reach parents who have allowed notifications in their browser. Not all parents will receive them — always follow up with an email for important closures.

A4 · Families Tab

Search, create, and manage all family accounts.

Searching for a Family

Type a name or email in the search box. Results are paginated 20 per page — use the Prev/Next buttons to browse.

Editing a Family

  1. Find the family and click ✏ Edit.
  2. Update parent name, email, phone, or Parent 2 details.
  3. To add a child, click + Add Child. Fill in name, date of birth, and optionally a room override.
  4. To remove a child row, click the ✕ button next to that child.
  5. Click Save.
Leave Room Override blank and the system assigns the child to the correct room automatically based on their date of birth.

Generating a New PIN

  1. Open the family's edit modal.
  2. Click New PIN (or New PIN 2 for the second parent).
  3. A 4-digit PIN appears — copy it and give it to the family.
  4. Click Save.
You cannot retrieve a PIN after closing the modal. Write it down before saving.

Locking / Unlocking Registration

  • Click 🔒 Lock Reg to prevent a family from adding new registrations (e.g., outstanding balance).
  • Click 🔓 Unlock Reg to restore their access.

Unlocking a Locked Login

If a parent enters the wrong PIN 5 times in a row, their login locks automatically. Click 🔓 Unlock Login next to their name to restore access.

Archiving & Restoring

  • Archive — hides the family from the active list. Use at year-end. Their data is preserved.
  • ↩ Restore — brings an archived family back to the active list.
  • Archive Summer Families — one-click button to archive all families enrolled only in Summer Camp.

Merging Duplicate Families

Click Merge on one of the duplicate records. Select the record to merge into and confirm. All registrations are moved to the surviving record.

Deleting a Family

Delete permanently removes the family and ALL their data including registrations. This cannot be undone.
  1. Click 🗑 Delete next to the family.
  2. Confirm the dialog. The family is gone immediately.

Importing Families (Bulk)

  1. Prepare a CSV or XLSX with columns: parent_name, parent_email, parent_phone, child_name, child_dob.
  2. Click ⬆ Import and upload your file.
  3. Review the preview table, then click Import to confirm.

Data Health Banner

A yellow banner appears when families have missing email addresses, phone numbers, or child dates of birth. Click Show Issues Only to filter to just those families.

A5 · Messages Tab

Incoming messages submitted by parents through the "Contact Us" form on the public site.

  • Unread messages are highlighted with a New badge.
  • Click the parent's email address to open your email client and reply directly.
  • Click Mark as Read once you've reviewed a message.
  • Click 📥 Archive to hide a message from the main view (it's not deleted).
  • Click 🗑 Delete to permanently remove a message.
  • Toggle Show Archived to review archived messages.
Delete is permanent. Archive is the safer option if you might need the message later.

A6 · Waitlist Tab

Manage families who have applied for a spot but aren't yet enrolled.

Reading the Waitlist

Each row shows the parent name, child name, room requested, how many days they've been waiting, and current status: Pending, Spot Offered, Accepted, or Archived. Offers past their deadline are flagged with ⚠️.

Offering a Spot

  1. Click 🎉 Offer a Spot next to the family.
  2. Set an offer deadline date (how long they have to respond).
  3. Optionally add the ProCare enrollment link and any notes for the family.
  4. Click Send Spot Offer. An email goes to the parent automatically.

After the Family Responds

  • If they accept: click ✓ Mark Accepted.
  • If they don't respond or decline: click Archive ▾, choose a reason, and confirm.
After archiving a declined family, you can re-offer to the next person on the list.

A7 · Reports Tab

Family Billing Report

  1. Pick a month from the dropdown.
  2. Click Generate.
  3. The report shows each family's full days, half days, change fees, sibling discounts, and amount owed.
  4. To override a family's amount (e.g. apply a credit): click the ✏ pencil on their row, enter the new amount, click Save.
  5. Click Export CSV to download a spreadsheet, or Print to open a printable version.
Automatic adjustments applied by the system: $5 change fee per date changed after the window closes · $10 sibling discount per additional child in the same family. These appear as separate line items.
Requires developer to change the $5 or $10 amounts.

Staff Schedule Report

  1. Pick a week start date.
  2. Click Generate.
  3. The report shows AM/PM shifts, enrolled child counts per room, and required staff based on your set ratios.
  4. Click 🪄 Auto-Fill Names to have the system suggest staff assignments based on recorded availability.
  5. Click Export to download as CSV.

A8 · Staffing Tab

Staff Roster

  • Click + Add Staff Member to add a new person. Fields: name, role, hourly rate or biweekly salary, room assignment, 4-digit clock-in PIN, hire date.
  • Click Edit on any row to update their details.
  • Click Deactivate to remove someone from scheduling and the kiosk without deleting their history. Click again to reactivate.
  • Click 🗑 Delete to permanently remove the staff member.
  • Toggle Show Inactive to see deactivated staff.
Deleting a staff member is permanent. Deactivate instead if you may need their clock-in history.

Staff Ratios

Set the maximum number of children one staff member may supervise in each room.

  1. Enter a number in each room's ratio field. Typical state minimums: Bear 4, Bee 4, Turtle/Goose/Owl 8.
  2. Click 💾 Save Ratios.

These ratios are used by the staff schedule report to flag understaffed days.

A9 · Finance Tab

Finance Dashboard

  1. Select a year and click Generate Dashboard.
  2. The dashboard shows monthly revenue, per-room breakdown, and a profit/loss trend chart.
  3. Click Year-over-Year to compare two consecutive program years side by side.

Expense Lines

Add your recurring monthly costs so the P&L view reflects real operating profit.

  1. Click + Add Expense.
  2. Enter a category name (e.g. "Utilities"), the monthly amount, and whether it's fixed or variable.
  3. Click Save. The expense appears in all P&L charts going forward.
  4. Click Edit or Delete on any row to update or remove it.

Modeling Tool

Simulate what revenue would look like if rates or enrollment changed — without touching real data. Enter hypothetical rates or capacity numbers and click Run Model to see the projected impact.

A10 · Settings Tab

Room Rates

  1. The rates table shows each room's full-day rate, half-day rate, weekly full rate, weekly half rate, and age range.
  2. Click into any field to edit the value.
  3. Click Save Rates. Changes take effect immediately for new registrations.
Weekly rates apply when a child books all 5 weekdays in a single week with the same day type. Leave blank to charge the per-day rate instead.

Summer Camp Visibility

Toggle Hide Summer Camp to remove the Summer Camp room from the parent registration page. Use this when summer enrollment is not yet open. Click 💾 Save.

Enrollment at Capacity

Toggle Enrollment at Capacity to display an "enrollment full" message on the parent portal across all rooms — without closing individual rooms in the calendar. Click 💾 Save.

Enrollment Forms

  1. Click Upload Form to add a PDF (health form, parent handbook, emergency contacts, etc.).
  2. Enter a name and optional description for the form.
  3. The form appears as a download link on the parent registration page immediately.
  4. Click 🗑 Delete to remove a form.

Waitlist Offer Links

Set the default ProCare enrollment URL and any supplementary links that are included in waitlist offer emails. Click 💾 Save Links.

A11 · Things That Update Automatically

You don't need to do anything for these — the system handles them on its own.

  • Room assignment — when a new child is added, the system assigns the correct room based on their date of birth.
  • Registration window — enrollment opens automatically at 9 AM on the 15th of each month.
  • Change fee — a $5 fee is added automatically when staff edit a care date after the registration window has closed.
  • Sibling discount — a $10 discount per additional child is applied automatically at billing time for families with more than one enrolled child.
  • Schedule confirmation emails — sent automatically to parents when they submit a registration.
  • Waitlist offer emails — sent automatically when you click "Send Spot Offer" in the Waitlist tab.
  • Push notifications — sent to subscribed parents when you click "🔔 Notify Parents" in the Care Calendar tab.

A12 · Things That Require a Developer

These cannot be changed through the admin portal — ask your developer (or ask Claude in a new session).

  • Room capacity numbers (e.g. changing Bear Room from 8 to 10)
  • The registration window schedule — currently the 15th at 9 AM Central
  • The $5 change fee or $10 sibling discount amounts
  • Adding or renaming rooms
  • Opening the Goose Room — currently "Coming Soon", requires a state inspection before a developer can set the capacity and activate it
  • The facility name, address, phone, or contact email on the public-facing pages
  • Email template content (beyond the offer notes field in the Waitlist tab)
  • The Privacy Policy text
  • Adding or removing admin accounts

A14 · Asking Claude to Make Changes

Start a new Claude Code session in the childcare-portal project folder and describe what you need. Be specific — say the current value and the new value.

Good examples:

  • "Change the Bear Room capacity from 8 to 10."
  • "Update the Owl Room half-day rate to $50."
  • "Add a closure for May 26, 2026 — Memorial Day."
  • "Change the registration window to open on the 10th instead of the 15th."
  • "Make the Goose Room active with a capacity of 12."
  • "Add the church office phone number (314-555-0100) to the contact page."

Tips

  • Always mention what it is now and what you want it to be.
  • For anything involving real family data (deletions, merges, bulk changes), ask Claude to show you the change before applying it.
  • If Claude proposes a change you're unsure about, just say "stop" or "don't do that" — it won't proceed without your approval.
  • Claude can read the codebase and explain what any button or feature does if you're unsure.
PART B — Parent Guide
📋 Share this guide with new families at enrollment.

B1 · How to Check Your Schedule

  1. Go to the MDO parent portal — your director will give you the link (it ends in /lookup.html).
  2. Enter the email address you used when you enrolled.
  3. Enter your 4-digit PIN — given to you by the MDO office when your account was created.
  4. Click Look Up My Schedule.
  5. Your confirmed care days for this month and next month appear, grouped by child.
If you don't know your PIN, contact the MDO office — PINs cannot be reset online. See section B6.

B2 · Reading Your Schedule

  • Each child has their own card, showing their classroom name.
  • Care days appear as small chips — Full means a full care day, ½ means a half day.
  • Days are grouped by month, with a subtotal for each month and a grand total at the bottom.
  • Only confirmed care days are shown — waitlisted days do not appear here.
If a day you expected to see is missing, contact the MDO office — it may still be on the waitlist.

B3 · Printing Your Schedule

  1. After looking up your schedule, click the Print button at the top of the results.
  2. Your browser's print dialog will open.
  3. To save a PDF copy, choose Save as PDF from the destination/printer options.

B4 · Downloading Your Data

You have the right to download a copy of all information we hold about your family. This is your right under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

  1. After looking up your schedule, scroll down to the Data & Privacy section.
  2. Click Download My Data.
  3. A file downloads to your device containing your family details, children's information, and your full registration history.

B5 · Requesting Account Deletion

You can ask us to delete your family's account and all associated records at any time.

  1. After looking up your schedule, scroll down to the Data & Privacy section.
  2. Click Request Account Deletion.
  3. Optionally enter a reason in the text box.
  4. Click Send Deletion Request.
  5. A confirmation message will appear. The office will process your request within 30 days.
Records required for financial or legal compliance may be kept for the minimum legally required period even after your account is deleted.

B6 · Forgot Your PIN or Locked Out?

PINs cannot be reset online. You must contact the MDO office to get a new PIN.
  • If you've forgotten your PIN, call or email the MDO office and they'll generate a new one for you.
  • If you enter the wrong PIN 5 times in a row, your login locks automatically for security.
  • To unlock your login, contact the office — they can unlock it from the admin portal immediately.

Office email: mdo@timothystl.org

B7 · Troubleshooting

  • "Email and PIN do not match" — Make sure you're using the exact email address you provided when enrolling. PINs are 4 digits only — no letters or spaces.
  • Page not loading — Try a different browser (Chrome or Safari work best) or check your internet connection.
  • Schedule looks wrong or a day is missing — Contact the office. A day may still be waitlisted, or there may be a data entry issue.
  • Can't see this month's dates — The portal shows the current month and next month only. For older records, download your data (see section B4).

For anything else, contact us at mdo@timothystl.org.
