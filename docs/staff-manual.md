# Staff User Manual
## Timothy Lutheran Church — Mother's Day Out
### Clock In / Clock Out Guide

---

## Overview

The Staff Clock In / Out page lets you record when you arrive and leave each day. Your times are used by the office to track hours and process payroll.

The clock-in page is accessible at `clockin.html`. It is designed to be used on a shared device (tablet or phone) kept at the center — you do not need to log in with a username or password.

---

## Your PIN

You clock in and out using a **4-digit PIN** assigned to you by your director or administrator. Your PIN is unique to you. Keep it private — do not share it with others.

If you do not know your PIN or need it changed, contact your director or the office administrator.

---

## Clocking In

1. Open `clockin.html` on the shared device.
2. The screen shows a numeric keypad. **Enter your 4-digit PIN** by tapping the number buttons.
   - The four dots at the top fill in as you enter each digit.
   - If you make a mistake, tap the **⌫** (backspace) button to erase the last digit.
3. After the 4th digit is entered, the system automatically looks up your record.
4. Your name and role will appear on screen, along with today's date.
5. Tap the large green **Clock In** button.
6. A confirmation message — *"Clocked in! Returning to PIN screen…"* — appears briefly, then the screen returns to the PIN entry pad automatically.

That's it. Your clock-in time has been recorded.

---

## Clocking Out

1. Open `clockin.html` on the shared device (or it may already be showing the PIN screen).
2. **Enter your 4-digit PIN**.
3. Your name will appear along with your clock-in time for today.
4. Tap the large navy **Clock Out** button.
5. A confirmation message — *"Clocked out. Have a great rest of your day!"* — appears on screen.
6. Your total hours worked for today are displayed.

---

## Reading the Status Screen

After entering your PIN, the screen shows:

| Field | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Your name and role** | Confirms the system recognized your PIN |
| **Today's date** | The current workday date |
| **Clocked In** | The time you clocked in today |
| **Clocked Out** | The time you clocked out (shown as — if still clocked in) |
| **Hours total** | Running total of hours worked today across all shifts |

---

## Working Multiple Shifts in One Day

If you leave and return on the same day (for example, a split shift), you can clock in and out more than once:

1. Clock out when you leave as normal.
2. When you return, enter your PIN again.
3. The screen will show your previous shift and a message: *"Shift recorded. Clock in again to start a new shift."*
4. Tap **Clock In** to start your new shift.

All shifts from the same day are totaled together in the **Hours total** field and in the payroll records.

---

## Sign Out (Without Clocking)

If you entered your PIN to check your status but do not need to clock in or out, tap **Sign Out** to return to the PIN screen without making any changes to your record.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

**"Invalid PIN. Please try again."**
You entered an incorrect PIN. Re-enter your PIN carefully. Make sure Caps Lock is not on if using a keyboard.

**Too many failed attempts — locked out**
After **5 incorrect PIN entries**, the device will be locked for **5 minutes** as a security measure. Wait for the timer shown on screen to reach zero, then try again. If you are locked out because you simply forgot your PIN, contact your director.

**"No open clock-in found"**
You tried to clock out but the system could not find an open clock-in record for today. This can happen if there was a technical problem when you clocked in. Contact your director or administrator so they can manually correct your hours.

**Error message on screen**
If you see an error in red text, note the exact message and let your director know. They can correct your hours manually through the admin panel.

**Clock In / Clock Out buttons both appear missing or disabled**
Try refreshing the page and entering your PIN again. If the problem continues, notify your director.

---

## Tips

- **Clock in as soon as you arrive** — before you start work.
- **Clock out before you leave** — even if you step out briefly and are done for the day.
- If you forgot to clock in or out, tell your director that same day so they can correct it promptly.
- Your clock-in times are the official record used for your paycheck. Accurate timekeeping helps ensure you are paid correctly.
- Do not enter another staff member's PIN, even to help them. Each person must record their own time.
