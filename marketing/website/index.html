Common Questions

Things families ask us.

Do I have to commit to the same days every week?
No — that's one of the things families love about MDO. You request days month by month based on what works for your schedule. There's no fixed weekly requirement.
How do full days and half days work?
Full day is 8:15 am to 5:00 pm. Half day is 8:15 am to 1:00 pm. You can mix and match on a day-by-day basis when you request your dates online.
What if a day is full when I try to book?
The portal shows live availability. Fully booked days will appear as unavailable. Contact the office if you need to be added to a waitlist for a specific date.
Is the program open to families outside the church?
Absolutely. Timothy Lutheran MDO welcomes all families, regardless of religious affiliation. Our faith values shape our culture of care — all children and parents are welcome here.
How do I get my 4-digit family PIN?
Your PIN comes from ProCare, our child management system. Log in at schools.procareconnect.com to look up your family's PIN. You can also search the portal by name or email if you don't have it handy. Contact us at mdo@timothystl.org if you need help.
My child is on the waitlist. What happens next?
When a spot opens in your child's age group, the office will reach out directly. You can also join the online waitlist through the care day portal for the fastest notification.
Bear Room — Birth to 12 Months

Infant Program: What to Know

Our infant room is a nurturing, peaceful environment where your baby is cared for with love, attention, and intention. We believe these early months are so special, which is why we focus on gentle, one-on-one care to help each child feel safe, secure, and truly known.

Throughout the day, your little one will enjoy meaningful interactions including songs, pat-a-cake, cuddles, and engaging moments that support early development. We take pride in creating a calm, loving space where your baby can grow, explore, and thrive at their own pace.

How does scheduling work for infants?
The infant program uses a set-day schedule rather than our flexible monthly request system. At initial enrollment you choose the specific days of the week your child will attend, and those days remain fixed for as long as your child is in the Bear Room. A minimum of two set days per week is required — this allows infants to build familiarity with their caregivers and daily routine. Once your child turns one and moves to the next classroom, they transition to the standard flexible schedule.
Can I add extra days beyond my set schedule?
You may request additional days in a given month if space is available in the infant room — just reach out to the office. Additional days are not guaranteed and are offered on a space-available basis only.
What do I need to bring for feeding?
Families are asked to send premade bottles each day to ensure feeding routines are smooth and tailored to your baby's needs. Formula must arrive premixed and ready to serve. Breast milk must be brought in labeled bottles or storage bags with your child's name and the date. Please label every bottle clearly with your child's full name. Staff will not mix or prepare formula on-site.
What diapering supplies should I send?
Please supply an adequate number of diapers for the day as well as any diaper cream your child uses. Wipes are provided by the center — you do not need to bring those.
How does nap time work in the infant room?
All infants in the Bear Room follow a center-set nap schedule. Every baby naps at the same time each day, on the schedule established by our staff. Individual on-demand nap schedules are not accommodated, as a consistent routine supports the development of all infants in the room.
When does my child move out of the infant room?
Children transition out of the Bear Room when they turn one year old. At that point they move to the Bee Room (12–24 months) and gain access to the flexible monthly scheduling system used throughout the rest of the MDO program.
