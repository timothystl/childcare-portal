A message from the Timothy Lutheran MDO Office

Dear MDO Families,

We are always looking for ways to make your experience at MDO smoother — from drop-off to paperwork. This spring we're excited to introduce our online care day request portal, replacing the paper calendar sign-up sheets with a simple, anytime-accessible system.

You can now choose your care dates, pick full-day or half-day options, and see available spots — all from your phone or computer, whenever it's convenient for you.

How it works

1

Find your family

Search by name, email, or your 4-digit family PIN on the portal. No account or password required.

2

Pick your days

Select any open dates on the calendar. Choose full-day or half-day for each. The portal shows real-time availability so you always know what's open.

3

Submit — you're done

Your request is confirmed instantly. We handle the roster on our end — no follow-up call needed.
