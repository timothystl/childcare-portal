Important Update: Online Day Registration Is Here

A message for our enrolled MDO families

Dear MDO Families,

We are excited to share a significant improvement to how you register for care days at Mother’s Day Out. Starting now, all day registration will take place through our new online portal instead of paper calendars and email requests.

Paper calendars get lost or misplaced, and emails can be easily overlooked — handling these also takes time away from direct child care and program operations. The new portal makes the process faster, clearer, and truly first come, first served for everyone.

How to Register

  1. Visit the portal Go to:
    mdo.timothystl.org
  2. Find your family & enter your PIN Search for your family by name or email, then enter your 4-digit PIN. Your PIN comes from your Procare account — the same system we use to manage enrollment. If you haven’t registered your family in Procare yet, please contact the office and we will get you set up.
  3. Select your children Choose which children you are registering. If your children need different schedules, you can submit one child at a time.
  4. Pick your care days on the calendar Browse the calendar and select the days you need. Days showing limited availability or no availability are updated in real time, so what you see reflects actual open spots.
  5. Choose Full Day or Half Day For all rooms except the infant room, you can choose which type of day works best for each date you select.
  6. Submit Review your selections and submit. You will receive a confirmation of your registered days.
Billing will continue to happen through Procare, just as it always has.

We believe this change will make day registration smoother and more fair for every family. Thank you for your patience as we make this transition, and thank you for being part of our MDO community.

With gratitude,
