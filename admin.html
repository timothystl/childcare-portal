🪑 Seat-Day Capacity Model

Plans around occupied seats per day, not enrolled headcount — with flexible schedules, one seat can serve more than one child across a week. Active children ≈ average occupied seats/day × 5 ÷ average booked days/child.

Click Generate Report to view the seat-day capacity model.

📈 Demand Forecast

Projects children per room per day for the weeks ahead, from the same-weekday average and a four-week moving average. Shows what is already booked alongside it, so the booking still to come is visible. Converts bookings into expected attendance once attendance is being marked.

Click Generate Forecast to project the weeks ahead.

🏖️ PTO Settings

The rate at which hourly staff accrue paid time off (hours earned per hour worked — e.g. 0.04 ≈ 1 hour of PTO earned per 25 hours worked). Applied automatically in the Payroll report. Salaried staff don't accrue PTO here.

Scheduling a new rate never changes the past — hours already worked keep accruing at whatever rate was in effect when they were worked. Past rates are locked and can't be edited; you can only remove a rate that hasn't taken effect yet.
