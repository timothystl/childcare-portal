Privacy Policy

Timothy Lutheran Church Mother's Day Out  ·  Effective March 2026

Timothy Lutheran Church Mother's Day Out ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of the families and children in our program. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1. Information We Collect

When you register for our program or create a family account, we collect:

  • Parent / Guardian information: full name, email address, phone number, and a numeric access PIN.
  • Child information: child's name, date of birth (used solely to assign the appropriate age-based classroom).
  • Care schedule data: requested care dates, day type (full/half day), and registration status.
  • Contact messages: any messages you send through the "Contact Us" form.

We do not collect payment card numbers or process payments through this portal. Billing is handled separately by the MDO office.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect exclusively to operate the MDO program:

  • Processing and managing your child's care registrations.
  • Sending schedule confirmation and change notification emails.
  • Generating classroom rosters and staffing assignments for MDO staff.
  • Producing billing summaries for the MDO administration.
  • Responding to questions submitted via the contact form.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our portal collects limited information about children under 13 (name and date of birth) solely to administer childcare services. This information is provided by a parent or legal guardian and is not used for any purpose other than operating the MDO program. We do not knowingly allow children to register or interact with the portal directly.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the United States.
  • Access to the database is protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Family PINs are stored as bcrypt hashes — never as reversible plain text.
  • Admin access requires email/password authentication with a 30-minute inactivity timeout.
  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS) enforced at the Cloudflare edge.

5. Data Retention

Registration records are retained for the current and prior program year to support billing reconciliation and attendance reporting. Records older than two program years may be purged at the discretion of the MDO director.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Download a copy of the data we hold about your family from the My Schedule portal (use the "Download My Data" button after logging in).
  • Correction — Request corrections to any inaccurate personal data by emailing mdo@timothystl.org.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your family's account and all associated records via the "Request Account Deletion" form in the My Schedule portal, or by emailing mdo@timothystl.org. We will process deletion requests within 30 days. Note that records required for legal or financial compliance may be retained for the minimum legally required period.
  • Opt-out of sale — We do not sell personal information. No opt-out is required.

7. Cookies and Tracking

This portal does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. The only local storage used is a session token maintained by our authentication provider (Supabase) to keep admins logged in, and a browser preference key to remember the last active admin dashboard tab. No analytics cookies are set on the parent-facing pages.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate this portal:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and serverless functions. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — CDN hosting and edge security. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts loaded from Google's CDN. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (schedule confirmations, waitlist offers). See Resend Privacy Policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or to exercise any of the rights described above:

  • Email: mdo@timothystl.org
  • Phone: Contact the church office at Timothy Lutheran Church
  • Address: Timothy Lutheran Church, St. Louis, MO

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the registration portal after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
