Why Timothy MDO

More than just childcare.

We believe the early years are precious. Our program is built around small class sizes, intentional play, and caring staff who know every child by name.

🏡

Community-rooted

MDO is woven into the fabric of the Lindenwood Park community. Many of our families return year after year because of the relationships they build here.

🌱

Small classrooms

Low child-to-staff ratios mean every child gets personal attention. Our classrooms are calm, predictable environments designed for little learners to thrive.

🗓️

Flexible scheduling

Full days and half days available Monday through Friday year round. Request only the days you need — no rigid weekly commitment required.

🤝

Faith-based environment

Our program is grounded in Christian values of kindness, care, and respect. All families are welcome regardless of faith background.

📲

Easy online scheduling

Our care day portal lets you pick dates, choose full or half day, and see real-time availability — from your phone, any time.

👩‍🏫

Experienced staff

Our educators are trained in early childhood development and many have been part of the MDO family for years. You'll know their names — and they'll know your child's.

Common Questions

Things families ask us.

Do I have to commit to the same days every week?
No — that's one of the things families love about MDO. You request days month by month based on what works for your schedule. There's no fixed weekly requirement.
How do full days and half days work?
Full Day is 8:15 am to 5:00 pm. Half Day is 8:15 am to 1:00 pm. You can mix and match on a day-by-day basis when you request your dates online.
What if a day is full when I try to book?
The portal shows live availability. Fully booked days will appear as unavailable. You can join the online waitlist through the care day portal for the fastest notification when a spot opens.
Is the program open to families outside the church?
Absolutely. Timothy Lutheran MDO welcomes all families, regardless of religious affiliation. Our faith values shape our culture of care — all children and parents are welcome here.
How do I get my 4-digit family PIN?
Your PIN is assigned when you enroll and shared with you by the office. If you can't remember it, you can also look up your family by name or email address in the portal.
My child is on the waitlist. What happens next?
When a spot opens in your child's age group, the office will reach out directly. You can also join the online waitlist through the care day portal for the fastest notification.
